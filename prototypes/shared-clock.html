Shared clock prototype

The question. Two people in a session share one clock. The goal is that their timers finish together, because the end of a phase is when the call turns on or off. Videos may drift a little. Does the model below get there without videos jumping more than once per press?

  • Commands are relative or fresh. Jump ±10s and pause/resume are relative to wherever you are. "Skip to the next phase" starts that phase fresh.
  • You see your own press at once. Your device applies it straight away. The Durable Object applies it when it arrives and sends the result to everyone.
  • A video moves once per command. The presser's video moves at the press, and everyone else's moves when the command reaches them. Nothing corrects a video afterwards. A new phase starts its playlist at the shared place.
  • Only the timer settles. When the DO's answer arrives, your timer takes the DO's end time, so a skip that reaches you late starts at 18.5s instead of 20s. The DO flips the phase itself, and in the real app that is also when the call opens or closes.
  • Server time without the wall clock. Each device counts with a steady timer and corrects its offset to server time from messages it already gets, when it is more than 100 ms off.

This page is throwaway. The middle card stands in for the Durable Object, and the outer cards stand in for two devices. Time runs for real, so the walkthroughs use short phases. Nothing is saved.
