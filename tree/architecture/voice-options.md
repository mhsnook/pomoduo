# Voice transport options for pomoduo

Researched 2026-09-10 against primary sources. Context: TanStack Start on Cloudflare Workers, Durable Object (DO) per room, voice-only, 2 people typical, up to about 6.

## Question this answers

What should carry the audio between participants: Cloudflare Realtime SFU (or RealtimeKit), a browser P2P mesh signalled through a DO, or a managed third party? Compared on price, signalling and TURN, DO fit, mic-only and mute semantics, mobile Safari and background tabs, ringing, and effort for one developer.

## Findings

### Option 1: Cloudflare Realtime SFU, and RealtimeKit

- Price: SFU and TURN cost $0.05/GB egress with one shared free tier of 1,000 GB/month; publishing to Cloudflare is free ([SFU pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/sfu/pricing/)). TURN is free when used with the SFU ([TURN](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/turn/)). RealtimeKit is separate: $0.0005 per audio-only participant-minute, no free tier listed ([RealtimeKit pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/realtimekit/pricing/), [overview table](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/index.md)). No Workers Paid plan requirement is stated anywhere (unconfirmed either way).
- Signalling: there is no Cloudflare signalling server and no official SDK for the raw SFU ("Cloudflare Realtime does not offer a SDK", [introduction](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/sfu/introduction/index.md)). Your backend holds the app secret and calls `sessions/new`, `tracks/new`, `renegotiate`, `tracks/close` over HTTPS ([API](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/sfu/https-api/)). A DO can own this: Cloudflare's own [meet](https://github.com/cloudflare/meet) (formerly orange) uses a PartyServer `ChatRoom` DO and the `partytracks` library, and [realtime-examples/video-room](https://github.com/cloudflare/realtime-examples/tree/main/video-room) is a Worker plus hibernating DO per room, marked Experimental.
- TURN: SFU is a public anycast endpoint so TURN is optional ("optional, but highly recommended", [partytracks](https://github.com/cloudflare/partykit/tree/main/packages/partytracks)). Credentials come from `POST .../turn/keys/$ID/credentials/generate-ice-servers` with a TTL, callable from a Worker ([generate credentials](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/turn/generate-credentials/)).
- Mic-only and mute: audio-only tracks are fine (Opus, G.711). Hard constraint: a track with no packets for 30 s is garbage collected, and sessions idle for 30 s time out ([limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/sfu/limits/)). So mute cannot be "stop sending". partytracks mutes by swapping in an inaudible 20 Hz synthetic track so packets keep flowing ([makeBroadcastTrack.ts](https://github.com/cloudflare/partykit/blob/main/packages/partytracks/src/client/makeBroadcastTrack.ts)). RealtimeKit: Voice preset, `disableAudio()`/`enableAudio()` ([audio calls](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/realtimekit/audio-calls/)).
- Mobile Safari and background: nothing Cloudflare-specific; see the WebKit notes under Option 2, they apply to all three options.
- Ringing: no Cloudflare primitive. Cloudflare documents Web Push from a DO using the `web-push` package under `nodejs_compat` ([Agents push guide](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agents/communication-channels/webhooks/push-notifications/)).
- Effort: raw SFU means HTTPS API plus partytracks (npm 0.0.x, RxJS based) plus your DO; copyable from meet. RealtimeKit is `@cloudflare/realtimekit-react` 2.x with a dashboard preset and a server-minted auth token ([quickstart](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/realtimekit/quickstart/)); lowest effort, but per-minute billing and an opinionated meeting model.

### Option 2: Browser P2P mesh, DO as signalling channel

- Price: signalling is DO traffic only. DOs run on the Workers Free plan (SQLite backend), 100k requests/day; incoming WebSocket messages bill at 20:1 and outgoing are free; hibernated sockets accrue no duration ([DO pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/platform/pricing/)). Media is peer to peer, so zero media cost unless relayed. Standalone Cloudflare TURN is $0.05/GB after the 1,000 GB free tier ([TURN FAQ](https://developers.cloudflare.com/realtime/turn/faq/)).
- Signalling: a DO relays SDP and ICE over WebSockets; Cloudflare's DO course builds exactly this ([make and answer WebRTC calls](https://developers.cloudflare.com/learning-paths/durable-objects-course/series/make-answer-webrtc-calls-6/)). PartyServer gives `onConnect`, `onMessage`, `broadcast`, `getConnection(id).send`, and `static options = { hibernate: true }` ([partyserver README](https://github.com/cloudflare/partykit/blob/main/packages/partyserver/README.md)). Deploys restart every DO and drop non-hibernated sockets ([websockets best practices](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/best-practices/websockets/)); the client needs reconnect and re-announce, though an established RTCPeerConnection survives a signalling drop.
- TURN: same credential endpoint as Option 1, minted by the DO. Relay is needed by whatever share of peers sit behind symmetric NAT; no primary source gives a number.
- Scaling: each peer uplinks N-1 encodes. libwebrtc Opus default is 32 kbps, 20 ms frames, DTX off ([audio_encoder_opus_config.h](https://webrtc.googlesource.com/src/+/refs/heads/main/api/audio_codecs/opus/audio_encoder_opus_config.h)), so 6 people is about 160 kbps up per peer plus overhead. Fine on desktop, noticeable on phones.
- Mic-only and mute: `getUserMedia({audio: true})`; `track.enabled = false` sends silence ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/MediaStreamTrack/enabled)); `sender.replaceTrack(null)` stops sending without renegotiation ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/RTCRtpSender/replaceTrack)). No 30 s timeout problem. DTX still needs SDP munging (`usedtx=1`, [RFC 7587](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7587.html), [open spec issue](https://github.com/w3c/webrtc-extensions/issues/120)).
- Mobile Safari and background (applies to every option): remote audio needs a user gesture unless the page is already capturing ([WebKit WebRTC post](https://webkit.org/blog/7763/a-closer-look-into-webrtc/)); AudioContext output is suspended when backgrounded, so play remote streams via an `<audio>` element ([bug 231105](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=231105)); the mic keeps running in a background Safari tab but stops in Home Screen web apps ([bug 226620](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=226620)); WebKit is still fixing background AudioSession behaviour in 2026 ([STP 248](https://webkit.org/blog/18162/release-notes-for-safari-technology-preview-248/)). Safari 18.4 adds a MediaSession capture-mute API ([Safari 18.4](https://webkit.org/blog/16574/webkit-features-in-safari-18-4/)).
- Ringing (applies to every option): Web Push from the DO. On iOS it only works for Home Screen web apps and permission needs a user gesture ([WebKit Web Push](https://webkit.org/blog/13878/web-push-for-web-apps-on-ios-and-ipados/)); Declarative Web Push from iOS 18.4 needs no service worker ([WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/16535/meet-declarative-web-push/)). The `web-push` npm package has an open Workers issue ([#718](https://github.com/web-push-libs/web-push/issues/718)); Web Crypto ports such as [webcrypto-web-push](https://github.com/block65/webcrypto-web-push) exist (third party). In-app ring over the room WebSocket covers anyone with the tab open; email or SMS link is the fallback.
- Effort: no SDK, but you own perfect negotiation ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebRTC_API/Perfect_negotiation)), `restartIce()`, per-peer connection state, and the DO reconnect story. Small for 2 peers, real work at 6.

### Option 3: Managed third party (Daily, LiveKit Cloud)

- Daily: 10,000 free participant-minutes/month, then $0.00099 per audio-only participant-minute, applied automatically when no video track is ever sent; no monthly fee ([pricing](https://www.daily.co/pricing/video-sdk/), [audio-only guide](https://docs.daily.co/guides/products/audio-only)). Signalling, SFU and TURN bundled (Daily uses Cloudflare and Twilio TURN, [allowlist](https://docs.daily.co/guides/privacy-and-security/corporate-firewalls-nats-allowed-ip-list)). Room and token are two `fetch` calls or a self-signed JWT, so a DO can mint them ([create room](https://docs.daily.co/reference/rest-api/rooms/create-room), [self-signed tokens](https://docs.daily.co/reference/rest-api/meeting-tokens/self-signing-tokens)). Room permission `canSend: ["audio"]` enforces voice only. `setLocalAudio(false)` mutes without stopping the track; `forceDiscardTrack` releases the mic ([setLocalAudio](https://docs.daily.co/reference/daily-js/instance-methods/set-local-audio)). React hooks package pulls in jotai ([daily-react](https://github.com/daily-co/daily-react)).
- LiveKit Cloud: Build plan is free, 5,000 minutes, 100 concurrent connections; Ship is $50/month for 150,000 minutes then $0.0005/min ([pricing](https://livekit.com/pricing), [quotas](https://docs.livekit.io/home/cloud/quotas-and-limits/)). No audio-only rate. Two people working 4 h/day is about 10,500 minutes/month, so this lands on the $50 plan. Token minting via `livekit-server-sdk` (jose based, Node/Deno/Bun listed, Workers not listed, [README](https://github.com/livekit/node-sdks/blob/main/packages/livekit-server-sdk/README.md)). `setMicrophoneEnabled(false)` mutes and keeps the publication ([publish docs](https://docs.livekit.io/transport/media/publish/)). Open issue: on Safari, tracks from participants who join while the tab is backgrounded stay silent until the tab is foregrounded ([#1751](https://github.com/livekit/client-sdk-js/issues/1751)).
- Neither offers a push or ringing primitive for the web; both need your own Web Push. Twilio Video reversed its EOL in Oct 2024 but was not researched further ([Twilio changelog](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/changelog/-twilio-video-will-remain-a-standalone-product)).

## Trade-offs

| | Cloudflare SFU + partytracks | RealtimeKit | P2P mesh + DO | Daily |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Marginal cost at this scale | Zero inside 1,000 GB | About $0.03 per 25 min block for 2 people | Zero, TURN relay only | Zero inside 10k min, then about $0.05 per 25 min block for 2 people |
| Extra vendor or account | No | No | No | Yes |
| Signalling owned by | Your DO | Cloudflare | Your DO | Daily |
| Mute semantics | Must keep packets flowing (30 s rule) | SDK handles it | Free choice | SDK handles it |
| 6 person room | SFU, one uplink per peer | Same | 5 uplinks per peer | SFU |
| Effort for one dev | Medium; copy from meet | Low | Low for 2, medium for 6 | Low |
| Lock-in | Cloudflare API, thin | Cloudflare SDK, heavy | None | Daily SDK |

Ringing and mobile Safari behaviour are identical across options; they are browser problems, not transport problems. The DO is a good signalling host in every option that needs one.

## Recommendation

Researcher's opinion, not a decision: use the Cloudflare Realtime SFU with partytracks and a PartyServer DO per room. It stays on the existing Cloudflare account and bill, is free at this scale including TURN, handles 6 people with a single uplink per peer, and Cloudflare's meet repo is a working template for exactly this DO plus partytracks shape. The one wrinkle, keeping packets flowing while muted because of the 30 s inactivity rule, is already solved inside partytracks. A P2P mesh is a fine fallback if partytracks proves too unstable at 0.0.x, and Daily is the fastest path if the priority shifts to shipping over owning the stack. RealtimeKit is the least attractive here because it charges per minute for a use case that fits inside the SFU free tier.
